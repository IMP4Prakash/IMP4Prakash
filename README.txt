PRAKASH CURSOR + TOUCH HERO

Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and the entire frames folder to the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repository.

Desktop: mouse movement controls direction.
Phone/tablet: touch and drag anywhere to control direction; releasing returns to center.
Pen/stylus is also supported through Pointer Events.

Important: The 64 frames were extracted from your supplied 8-second character.mp4. Because generated videos do not always follow a mathematically perfect circular direction order, if a direction feels reversed or offset after deployment, the frame-angle mapping can be calibrated in script.js without regenerating the site.
