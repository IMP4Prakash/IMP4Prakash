Watch together. Respect each other.

These guidelines apply to hosts and guests in every room.

Respect participants

No harassment, hate, threats, unwanted sexual content, impersonation or spam. Follow the host’s room decisions. Hosts can pause chat, limit camera access, remove participants and end a room.

Protect privacy

Share room codes only with trusted people. Do not disclose someone’s personal information, record a call or share screenshots without their permission. You can turn your microphone or camera off and leave at any time.

Use content responsibly

Only share content you have permission to access and share. Follow each video provider’s rules. Xparty does not bypass subscriptions, region restrictions or digital rights protection.

What the room processes

The room service processes your nickname, messages, playback actions and temporary uploaded files. Private messages are routed to the selected participant. Calls use WebRTC; media travels between participants or through a relay when one is configured. External video services have their own privacy practices.

Your choices

Essential browser storage remembers room recovery, privacy choices and your agreement acceptance. Optional preferences remember themes and language. No analytics service is installed. Change storage preferences from Settings. Clearing browser storage removes this device’s saved choices and rejoin credentials.

Account features require a separately configured provider. Where enabled, Profile provides account deletion. Account data and temporary guest-room data are separate. Hosting restarts may clear guest rooms; playback and calls depend on devices, network conditions and video providers.
