About Xparty

Different places. Same moment.

A code-only place to watch YouTube or a video from one device, talk and chat together. No email sign-in.

  • Up to 10 room members; four voice/video call seats.
  • Choose Host Approval, Host Only or Shared Control from the key menu. Hosts manage entry, requests and camera permissions.
  • Type @ and choose a person to send a private message. Other room members cannot receive it.
  • When the room owner exits, an online guest becomes temporary host. The owner regains control when returning with their saved browser session.
  • End room removes everyone. Rooms survive refresh but a server restart can still clear them.
  • Local videos up to 250 MB are temporarily uploaded to the room server and downloaded by guests. YouTube availability and buffering depend on YouTube and your connection.

Privacy & your data

Xparty is a PKX404 project. Guest rooms do not require an account. Room messages, codes and temporary shared files are processed by the room server. Call media normally travels between participants; a configured relay may carry it.

Optional accounts use Supabase to verify your email or phone. We store your display name, optional age and photo, friendships, private messages, and call signaling to provide those features. Your email and phone are not shown to friends. Your name and photo are visible to accepted friends.

Google access is optional and requested separately for YouTube playlists and Drive files. Sharing a Drive video uploads a temporary copy to your room server with your confirmation. Google and the selected video provider receive requests when you use their services.

You can edit your profile, remove your photo, or delete your account and associated Xparty database records in Profile. Deletion cannot remove copies saved by another person or provider backups/logs retained under their own policies. Guest room data is separate from account data.

Accounts are currently for adults aged 18 and over. Do not submit sensitive information or share room codes publicly. Contact the project owner through PKX404’s website.

Room chat attachments are shared only with this room or your selected recipient. Each file is limited to 20 MB and removed when the room ends, when it leaves chat history, after 24 hours, or when the server restarts.
